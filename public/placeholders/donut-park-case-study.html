Case study

Donut Park PH: 54 to 220 branches in 11 months.

A Philippine donut franchise where the system, not the demand, was the ceiling, until the backend could carry the growth.

ClientDonut Park PH
Timeline11 months as COO
Outcome54 to 220 branches

01The problem

They were at 54 branches when I joined as COO. The bottleneck wasn't demand, it was the system: accounting, warehouse, and supply chain, plus an operations team that didn't have clear processes to work from.

02The plan

Build the backend a franchise this size actually needs, warehouse, supply chain, ordering, and accounting, then put the SOPs in the team's hands so growth stops depending on any one person.

Warehouse & supply chain→ Accounting system→ Ordering system→ SOPs deployed

03What was built

The systems delivered across the franchise.
  • Warehouse inventory system
  • Supply chain system
  • Ordering system
  • Accounting system
  • Operations systems, with SOPs deployed to the full team

04Results

Scaled from 54 to 220 branches in just 11 months. The backend now onboards more than 15 new franchisees a month without breaking.
