Case study

Bernardino Private Hospital: 1 to 3 days, now 20 to 30 minutes.

A 3-branch hospital group with skilled physicians, well-trained staff, and modern equipment, affiliated with the Philippine Health Association and accredited by PhilHealth.

ClientBernardino Private Hospital
TimelineMulti-branch rollout
OutcomeProcess time cut by up to 95%

01The problem

The hospital wasn't heavily exposed to its local and digital presence, from a rough process on social media to its local SEO, making it difficult to welcome more patients across all 3 branches.

02The plan

Work with the marketing department to simplify and automate as much of the registration process, onboarding, and direct Facebook campaigns as possible, in place of hiring someone for $5,000 to $8,000 a month.

Registration→ Onboarding→ Facebook campaigns→ Automated system

03What was built

The pieces delivered across all 3 branches.
  • Simplified, automated registration process
  • Direct Facebook campaign integration
  • Operations and marketing team training, in place of a $5,000 to $8,000 a month hire

04Results

What used to take 1 to 3 days now takes only 20 to 30 minutes max, across all 3 branches.
