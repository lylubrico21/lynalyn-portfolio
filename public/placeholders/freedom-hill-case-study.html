Case study

Freedom Hill Real Estate: from bottleneck to 180 clients.

A North Carolina land investment company helping landowners and investors buy and sell vacant land, until its own agent became the bottleneck.

ClientFreedom Hill Real Estate
TimelineMulti-month engagement
Outcome20 to 50 clients/month to about 180

01The problem

Adrien was managing the North Carolina branch with about 20 to 50 clients a month when he became the massive bottleneck in the company. Even with a team behind him, he was still heavily involved in client management, straining operations, marketing, and converting sales.

02The plan

Start with the onboarding process, transformed so it could be delegated to the team. Add systems automation for better reporting, communication, and free time. Then build a CRM that does everything: funnels, campaigns, workflows, and a KPI dashboard for 90 days of ad performance.

Delegated onboarding→ Systems automation→ CRM + chatbots→ 180 clients

03What was built

The pieces delivered to take Adrien out of the bottleneck.
  • Delegated onboarding process for the team
  • Systems automation for reporting, communication, and free time
  • A done-for-you CRM: funnels, campaigns, and workflows, with leads and surveys monitored daily
  • Facebook, Instagram, website, and Google My Business integrated so no lead is missed
  • KPI dashboard for 90 days of ad performance
  • Smart chatbots across the website and socials

04Results

Because of the systems automation and the done-for-you CRM, Adrien is now sitting at about 180 clients, qualified with the help of the smart chatbots integrated into the system, website, and social platforms.
